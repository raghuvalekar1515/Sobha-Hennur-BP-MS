The township is laid out along a long site. Residential Blocks 1 to 5 follow the main spine and Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a loop road to the south and east, between parks, open spaces, an amenity block, civic amenities and a future development parcel, with a lake edge along the east.

How the Sobha Hennur site is arranged

Read the plan as a journey from west to east. You enter at the main access on the west, past the commercial precinct. The main spine then carries you past Residential Blocks 1 to 5, before a loop road turns to the south and east and links Blocks 6 to 12. The lake edge closes the site on the east.

Parks, open spaces, the amenity block and the civic amenities sit between the blocks. The aerial view above illustrates the plan; the detailed site plan comes with the launch.

The plan labels the residential blocks from 1 to 12, while the project counts 13 towers in all. Which tower stands in which block, and how many floors each one has, will be clear from the detailed plans at launch.

Sobha Hennur blocks along the main spine

Blocks 1 to 5 follow the main spine from the west. They are the homes nearest the main access and the commercial precinct, which puts the shops, restaurants and offices closest to the door.

Being near an entrance also means more comings and goings past your tower. If you prefer quiet, visit at a busy hour and see how the spine feels; if you value convenience, these blocks keep the township’s errands within easy reach.

Sobha Hennur blocks around the loop road

Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a loop road to the south and east. They sit deeper inside the site, further from the main access and nearer the lake edge along the east.

For a household that values the parks and the water’s edge more than the shops, these blocks deserve a close look. Ask which blocks will carry your home type, since the master plan does not show that yet.

Sobha Hennur open spaces, amenity block and civic land

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, spread as parks and open spaces between the blocks. The amenity block and the land for civic amenities are placed among them.

The plan also marks a future development parcel. What it will hold is not detailed yet. If your tower would face it, ask what is planned there, since it may change your view and the activity next door in later years.

How to read the Sobha Hennur master plan

  1. Find the main access on the west and follow the main spine east.
  2. Locate your block, numbered from 1 to 12, and see how far it is from the main access and the commercial precinct.
  3. Mark the parks, the amenity block and the clubhouse, and trace how you would walk to them.
  4. Follow the loop road, since vehicles will use it to reach Blocks 6 to 12.
  5. Look at the edges of the site: the lake edge on the east and the future development parcel.
  6. Remember that the aerial view is illustrative; positions and distances are fixed only on the detailed plan.

Choosing a position on the Sobha Hennur plan

Your block shapes daily life: how far you walk to the shops, which park you use and what you see from the balcony. With towers of up to 42 floors, the floor matters too, since a higher home may look out over the parks or the lake edge.

Before allotment, put your priorities in order: near the main access, near the lake edge, near a park, or a high floor. Then ask the sales team which blocks and floors match your home type.
