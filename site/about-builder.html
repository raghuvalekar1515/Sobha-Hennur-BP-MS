How Sobha Limited builds

Sobha Limited builds end to end. It runs its own contracting and its own manufacturing, which covers interiors, glazing, metal works and concrete products, so much of what goes into a building is made and fitted by the company itself. The idea behind it is self-reliance.

For a buyer, the practical point is accountability: when one company handles the structure, the glazing and the interiors, there is one party to answer for the finished home. Ask the sales team which parts of the Sobha Hennur homes, such as the interiors or the glazing, will come from Sobha’s own manufacturing, and ask to see sample finishes, such as a window frame or a door, so you can judge the workmanship before launch.

Sobha Limited segments and product range

The company works in four segments: residential, contractual, non-residential and commercial. Its product range takes in apartments, villas, row houses, plotted developments, commercial spaces and townships.

Sobha Hennur draws on several of these at once. It is a township of apartments, 4,463 of them in nine home types, with a commercial precinct of offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants on the same land. A buyer here chooses a home inside a larger neighbourhood that is planned as one.

Where Sobha Limited builds

Sobha Limited builds in 14 cities across India: Bengaluru, Chennai, Coimbatore, GIFT City Gandhinagar, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Kochi, Kozhikode, Mumbai, Mysuru, Pune, Thiruvananthapuram and Thrissur.

Outside Bengaluru, its projects include SOBHA Altus in Sector 106, Gurugram, SOBHA Nesara in Pune and SOBHA Lake Edge in Thrissur.

Sobha Limited in Bengaluru

Bengaluru is among the cities where Sobha builds. Its projects in the city include SOBHA Neopolis at Panathur, SOBHA Windsor at Whitefield, SOBHA Oakshire at Devanahalli, SOBHA Townpark and SOBHA OneWorld.

Sobha Hennur adds a township on the north-east side of the city, in the Hennur Road corridor across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages. Visiting another SOBHA project in Bengaluru before launch lets you see the company’s homes and finishes for yourself.

What Sobha Hennur adds to Sobha’s work

Sobha Hennur is planned at township scale: about 50.45 acres, 13 towers of up to 42 floors and 4,463 homes, with 5,029 parking spaces and about 37% of the land as green cover. Its homes run from a serviced 1 BHK to a 4 BHK with four bathrooms, with a 2 BHK + Home Office as the closest match to a 2.5 BHK, and most of them are 3 BHK.

It is in pre-launch, with details as of 17 October 2026. Prices, areas and the payment plan of each home type come at launch, so for now buyers weigh the developer’s record and the project’s own published facts.
