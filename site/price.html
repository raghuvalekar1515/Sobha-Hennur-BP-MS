Reading the Sobha Hennur indicative price

The figure of about ₹1.86 Cr belongs to a home of about 1,940 sq ft. It shows where the project sits, but it is not the price of a named home type, and the type it refers to is not stated.

Two cautions help. Do not divide the price by the area and apply that rate to another home: you do not yet know which area measure the 1,940 sq ft uses, and a rate can change with the type, the tower and the floor. And remember that each of the nine home types gets its own line on the price list, so a 1 BHK buyer and a 4 BHK buyer should each wait for the line that matches their home.

Sobha Hennur price and the area it is charged on

Apartment prices are often quoted on super built-up area, which adds a share of the walls, balconies and common spaces to the floor inside the home. Carpet area is only the usable floor within the walls, so the same home can carry two quite different figures.

Sobha Hennur’s shared spaces include a clubhouse, an amenity block, parks and landscaped gardens, and how much of them is counted into each home’s area changes the figure on the price list. When the list comes, ask which measure each area uses and request the carpet area of every home you shortlist, so you can compare it fairly with a home in another project.

What the Sobha Hennur cost sheet will show

A cost sheet sets out the full price of one specific home, line by line. Ask for the sheet of the exact home type, tower and floor you are considering, and look for these items:

  • The base price, and the area measure it is charged on.
  • Any charge that changes with the floor or the view, which matters in towers of up to 42 floors.
  • Car parking: 5,029 spaces are planned, so ask whether a space comes with your home or is priced on its own.
  • Clubhouse or amenity charges, if any, and what they cover.
  • Maintenance deposits or advance maintenance, and the months they cover.
  • Taxes, plus the stamp duty and registration that are payable over the price.
  • For the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment, any service or management charge linked to the serviced format.

Add the lines up yourself and compare totals, not base rates.

Sobha Hennur expression of interest before launch

Before a launch, buyers can register an expression of interest: you tell the sales team which home type you want and, in some cases, pay an amount that holds your place for allotment. It is not a purchase. The home, its price and the payment plan are fixed only when you accept a cost sheet and sign the paperwork.

At Sobha Hennur, an early expression of interest counts most where a group is small. The serviced 1 BHK has 100 homes and each 4 BHK format 166, against 1,428 homes in the 3 BHK Luxe. If any amount is asked for, find out in writing whether it is refunded if you decide not to go ahead.

This website records interest only. It does not accept bookings or collect payments.

Paying for a Sobha Hennur home in stages

Homes bought before completion are commonly paid for under a construction-linked plan: a booking amount, a payment at the agreement, and then instalments as the building reaches agreed stages, such as the foundation, groups of floor slabs and the finishing work.

Sobha Hennur’s towers rise to as many as 42 floors, so a slab-linked schedule can run to many steps. When the plan is shared with the price list, check whether each instalment is tied to your own tower’s progress or to a calendar date, and how you will be told that a stage is complete.

Home loan basics for a Sobha Hennur home

Most buyers fund part of the price with a home loan. A bank sets the amount it will lend from your income, your existing loans and the value of the home, and you pay the rest, your own contribution, from savings.

Ask for an in-principle sanction before launch, so you know your budget when the Sobha Hennur price list arrives. For a home under construction, a bank usually releases the loan in parts, in step with the payment plan, and you pay interest only on what has been released until the full loan is drawn. Keep the cost sheet and the payment plan of your exact home with you when you apply.

Before you pay a token for a Sobha Hennur home
