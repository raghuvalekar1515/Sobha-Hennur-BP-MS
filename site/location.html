The land lies across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk. It belongs to the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru but is not on Hennur Road itself, so allow for the approach from the main road when you plan a visit.

Sobha Hennur for work and the daily commute

For the daily commute, Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km away. Bhartiya City, the closest landmark in the distance table, is about 3.4 km away.

Some working days may not need the main road at all. Sobha Hennur plans offices in its own commercial precinct at the main access on the west of the site, business and meeting rooms among its shared spaces, and the 2 BHK + Home Office for days spent at home.

Sobha Hennur for schools, college and healthcare

For school-going children, VIBGYOR High School, Hennur is about 6.1 km away. For a college student, Kristu Jayanti University is about 4.9 km away. Within the township, learning spaces with reading tables and bookshelves are planned for study close to home.

For healthcare, Cratis Hospital on Hennur Bagalur Main Road is about 5.4 km away and Manipal Hospital, Hebbal about 12.4 km. A health and first-aid room is also planned among the shared spaces of the township.
