Sobha Hennur Location

Sobha Hennur sits in the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru, across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages. Daily life is close: Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away, Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km and VIBGYOR High School Hennur about 6.1 km, while Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km and Kempegowda International Airport about 20.9 km.
