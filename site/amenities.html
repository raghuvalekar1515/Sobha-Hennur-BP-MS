Sobha Hennur clubhouse and amenity block

The clubhouse is pictured as a lounge with a double-height window wall that opens onto the gardens. The amenity block is planned separately, among the parks and open spaces of the master plan, as a second place for shared facilities.

Which sports and fitness facilities sit inside the clubhouse, and how its floors are divided, will be confirmed at launch. When you ask for the brochure, ask for the clubhouse plan too, and ask when each shared facility will open compared with the handover of your tower.

Sobha Hennur everyday services within the township

Several small services save a trip out. A banking service point, shown with an ATM and a counter, handles routine banking. A resident help desk with parcel lockers looks after deliveries, a real convenience across 13 towers of up to 42 floors.

Space is also set aside for post and communication, and a health and first-aid room is planned within the township. For hospital care, Cratis Hospital on Hennur Bagalur Main Road is about 5.4 km away.

Sobha Hennur spaces for learning, work and indoor play

Learning spaces with reading tables and bookshelves give children and adults a quiet place to study inside the township. Business and meeting rooms let residents hold a meeting without travelling, which fits well with the 2 BHK + Home Office homes.

Indoor games, pictured with table tennis and carrom, give children and adults somewhere to play close to home when the weather keeps them in.

Sobha Hennur parks, gardens and green cover

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover. Parks and open spaces run between the towers, with landscaped gardens, a garden walk between lawns and trees, and a sports court and play lawn under shade trees, as the gallery shows.

A tree-planting programme is part of the plan, and the master plan shows a lake edge along the east of the site. When you choose a tower, look at which parks and gardens are nearest to it.

Sobha Hennur shops, restaurants and civic land

The commercial precinct at the main access on the west brings offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants inside the township. For residents, daily errands and a meal out need not mean leaving the site.

Land is also kept for civic amenities. What will be built on it is not detailed yet, so ask about it if a particular civic facility matters to you.

Sobha Hennur water and sustainability

Waste water is treated on site in a sewage treatment plant that uses the activated sludge process. Dual plumbing means two separate sets of pipes, one for fresh water and one for treated water, which is how the treated water can be reused on site.

Rainwater harvesting is planned as well, alongside the green cover and the tree-planting programme. Ask the sales team how treated water will be used around your tower, for example in the gardens.

Sobha Hennur amenities summary

Sobha Hennur plans a clubhouse, an amenity block and spaces for learning, health, banking, parcels, indoor games and business meetings; a commercial precinct of shops, restaurants, offices and service apartments; civic amenity land; and parks and gardens across about 37% of the land, with on-site sewage treatment, dual plumbing and rainwater harvesting. Before you book, ask:

  • Which facilities are inside the clubhouse and the amenity block?
  • Will the shops, restaurants and offices be open to the public as well as to residents?
  • What will maintenance cost, and which shared spaces does it cover?
  • Is a clubhouse or amenity charge part of the cost sheet?

Sobha Hennur amenities FAQs

Does Sobha Hennur have a clubhouse?

Yes. A clubhouse and an amenity block are planned; the facilities inside each are confirmed at launch.

Will there be shops within Sobha Hennur?

Yes. Shops and restaurants are planned in the commercial precinct at the main access on the west of the site, with offices and service apartments.

Is there a place for children to study at Sobha Hennur?

Learning spaces are planned among the shared facilities, along with indoor games such as table tennis and carrom.

How much green space does Sobha Hennur have?

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, landscaped gardens and a tree-planting programme.

How is water managed at Sobha Hennur?

Sewage is treated on site using the activated sludge process, the treated water is reused through dual plumbing, and rainwater is harvested.
