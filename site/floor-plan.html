Sobha Hennur 1 BHK and serviced 1 BHK

The compact end of Sobha Hennur has two formats. The 1 BHK, with 301 homes, is a self-contained home with one bedroom, a living and dining room, a kitchen and a balcony. The 1 BHK Serviced Apartment, with 100 homes, shows the same set of rooms on its layout, in a serviced format.

How the serviced homes will be run, and what that costs, is confirmed at launch. Ask about it before choosing between the two, especially if you plan to rent the home out rather than live in it. Either suits a single professional, or a couple who want a compact first home in the Hennur Road corridor.

Sobha Hennur 2 BHK: Luxe, Grande and Home Office

There are three 2 BHK formats with a similar number of homes: 318 in the 2 BHK Luxe, 310 in the 2 BHK Grande and 310 in the 2 BHK + Home Office. The Luxe and Grande layouts both show two bedrooms, a living and dining room, a kitchen and a balcony; how they differ in area is set out at launch.

The 2 BHK + Home Office adds a separate office room to the two bedrooms, which makes it the closest match to a 2.5 BHK. A 2.5 BHK is a two-bedroom home with a smaller extra room. Here that room is planned as an office, which suits someone who works from home on some days and wants a door to close on calls, without moving up to a 3 BHK.

Sobha Hennur 3 BHK: Luxe and Grande

Most homes at Sobha Hennur are 3 BHK: 1,428 in the 3 BHK Luxe and 1,298 in the 3 BHK Grande. Both layouts show three bedrooms, a living and dining room and a kitchen. The Grande layout shows a wide balcony, where the Luxe shows a balcony.

With so many 3 BHK homes across the towers, a 3 BHK buyer has the most choice of tower, floor and position in the master plan. Use that choice: decide what matters to you, whether it is the parks, the lake edge on the east or the main access on the west, before allotment opens.

Sobha Hennur 4 BHK: three or four bathrooms

The 4 BHK residences come in two formats, with 166 homes each. The 4 BHK Luxe has four bedrooms and three bathrooms. The 4 BHK Grande has four bedrooms and four bathrooms, and its layout shows balconies rather than a single balcony.

The difference is practical. Four bathrooms for four bedrooms leaves room for each bedroom to have its own, which suits a household with parents living in or grown-up children. With three, at least one bathroom is shared. Check which bedrooms have attached bathrooms on the detailed plans.

How to read a Sobha Hennur floor plan

  1. Start with the north arrow and see which way the living room and the balcony face.
  2. Read each room size, usually given as length by width, and check that a bed and a wardrobe fit in each bedroom.
  3. Count the bathrooms and see which open from a bedroom and which from the common area; this is the main difference between the 4 BHK Luxe and Grande.
  4. Find the kitchen and look for a utility or wash area beside it.
  5. Note the doors and windows, since they decide where furniture can go.
  6. Read the area statement and check whether it gives carpet area, super built-up area or both.

The 3D layouts on this page help you picture the rooms; the dimensioned plans released at launch are the ones to rely on when you sign.

Carpet area on a Sobha Hennur plan

The room sizes on a plan add up to roughly the carpet area: the floor inside the walls that you actually live on. A price list often shows a larger figure, the super built-up area, which also counts walls, balconies and a share of common spaces such as lobbies and the clubhouse.

The one area published for Sobha Hennur so far, about 1,940 sq ft beside the indicative price, does not say which measure it is. Ask for both figures for any home you shortlist, and compare carpet areas when you set a Luxe against a Grande, or a Sobha Hennur home against one elsewhere.

Choosing a Sobha Hennur home type for your household

  • A first home, or a home to rent out: the 1 BHK or the serviced 1 BHK.
  • A couple who both go to work: a 2 BHK Luxe or Grande, with Manyata Tech Park about 9 km away.
  • Working from home on some days: the 2 BHK + Home Office.
  • A family with school-going children: a 3 BHK, with VIBGYOR High School, Hennur about 6.1 km away.
  • Parents living in, or a larger family: a 4 BHK, the Grande if every bedroom needs its own bathroom.

Whichever type you lean towards, wait for the area of that exact home before you decide between two neighbouring types.
