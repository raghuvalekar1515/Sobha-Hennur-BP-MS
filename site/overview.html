Sobha Hennur Project Overview

Sobha Hennur brings a complete Sobha neighbourhood to the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru, across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli. Its 4,463 homes rise in 13 towers of up to 42 floors on about 50.45 acres, in nine home types: a serviced 1 BHK and a 1 BHK, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, 3 BHK homes in Luxe and Grande formats, and 4 BHK residences with three or four bathrooms. Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants are planned within the township, with a clubhouse, parks and about 37% of the land as green cover.

Sobha Hennur is a township, not a single building

Sobha Hennur is planned as a neighbourhood of its own. About 50.45 acres carry 13 towers of up to 42 floors and 4,463 homes, and the same land holds offices, service apartments, shops, restaurants, a clubhouse, an amenity block and civic amenity land.

That changes how you choose. At a single-tower project the main decision is the home. Here you also pick a tower and a place in a long site plan, and that place decides how far you walk to the shops, the parks or the main access. The master plan page walks through the layout block by block.

Scale also shows in the services. With 5,029 parking spaces planned and about 37% of the land set aside as green cover, the shared spaces are a large part of what a Sobha Hennur home includes, so they deserve as much attention as the rooms inside it.

Sobha Hennur township: homes beside shops and offices

The commercial precinct stands at the main access on the west of the site, with offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants. Residents pass it on the way in and out, so errands and a meal out can stay within the township.

Alongside the clubhouse and the amenity block, the shared spaces include rooms for learning, a health and first-aid room, a banking service point, a resident help desk for parcels, indoor games and business and meeting rooms. The amenities page explains what each one is for.

Sobha Hennur location in brief

The project lies across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk, within the Hennur Road corridor rather than on Hennur Road itself. Bhartiya City is the nearest landmark in the distance table, at about 3.4 km.

The drive to Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km, and to Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1, about 20.9 km. The location page groups every distance by work, study, health and travel, with a link to the map.
