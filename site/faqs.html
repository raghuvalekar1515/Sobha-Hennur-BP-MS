Sobha Hennur FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about “Sobha Hennur” project.

What is Sobha Hennur?

Sobha Hennur is an upcoming Sobha township in the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru: 4,463 homes in nine home types, from serviced 1 BHK apartments to 4 BHK residences, in 13 towers on about 50.45 acres, with offices, shops and restaurants within. It is in pre-launch, so register now for priority access.

Is Sobha Hennur on Hennur Road?

It is in the Hennur Road residential corridor, across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East, rather than on the road itself. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away and Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km.

Which home types can I choose from?

Nine: 1 BHK Serviced Apartment (100 homes), 1 BHK (301), 2 BHK Luxe (318), 2 BHK Grande (310), 2 BHK + Home Office (310), 3 BHK Luxe (1,428), 3 BHK Grande (1,298), 4 BHK Luxe with three bathrooms (166) and 4 BHK Grande with four bathrooms (166).

How much does a home at Sobha Hennur cost?

The indicative price is about ₹1.86 Cr for about 1,940 sq ft, with stamp duty and registration extra. Prices for each home type will be announced at launch; register your interest to receive the price list first.

How far is it from Manyata Tech Park and the airport?

Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km away and Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1, about 20.9 km. Thanisandra Railway Station is about 6.5 km away and Yelahanka Junction about 9.6 km.

What will the township include?

A clubhouse and an amenity block; space for learning, health, banking, post and communication, recreation and business; offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants; and parks and landscaped open spaces across about 37% of the land.

Why choose a Sobha home?

Sobha Limited, founded in 1995 by PNC Menon, builds end to end with its own contracting and manufacturing, from interiors to glazing, metal works and concrete products, a philosophy of self-reliance behind its quality and craftsmanship. It builds in 14 cities across India; its Bengaluru homes include SOBHA Neopolis, SOBHA Townpark and SOBHA OneWorld.
