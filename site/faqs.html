Frequently Asked Questions about “Sobha Hennur” project.

Sobha Hennur About the Project

What is Sobha Hennur?

Sobha Hennur is an upcoming Sobha township in the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru: 4,463 homes in nine home types, from serviced 1 BHK apartments to 4 BHK residences, in 13 towers on about 50.45 acres, with offices, shops and restaurants within. It is in pre-launch, so register now for priority access.

How big is Sobha Hennur?

Sobha Hennur covers about 50.45 acres. On that land, 13 towers of up to 42 floors hold 4,463 apartments, with 5,029 parking spaces planned and about 37% of the land kept as green cover. The same site also carries a commercial precinct, a clubhouse, an amenity block and civic amenity land.

What stage is Sobha Hennur at today?

It is in pre-launch, with the details on this website current as of 17 October 2026. Already known: the location, the land area, the towers, the nine home types and how many homes of each, the green cover, the parking and an indicative price. Still to come at launch: the price and area of each home, the detailed floor plans and the payment plan. Registering now puts you on the list to receive them first.

What does pre-launch mean for a buyer?

Before launch, a project’s land and plan are announced but its price list is not yet out. Buyers can register an expression of interest, telling the sales team which home type they want, so they hear first when allotment opens. Registering does not buy a home: the home, its price and its terms are settled later, on paper.

Is Sobha Hennur only an apartment project?

No. Sobha Hennur is planned as a township. Besides the apartments, a commercial precinct at the main access on the west holds offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants, and land is set aside for civic amenities. The master plan also marks a future development parcel, though its use has not been detailed. For residents, an errand, a meal out or a day at the office need not mean leaving the site.

How is the Sobha Hennur site laid out?

The site is long, with its main access on the west beside the commercial precinct. A main spine runs east past Residential Blocks 1 to 5, and a loop road then turns south and east to serve Blocks 6 to 12. Parks and the amenity block sit between the blocks, and a lake edge runs along the east. The plan numbers the residential blocks up to 12 while the project counts 13 towers, so check which tower stands where on the detailed plan at launch. The master plan page walks through it.

Sobha Hennur Homes, Sizes and Plans

Which home types can I choose from?

Nine: 1 BHK Serviced Apartment (100 homes), 1 BHK (301), 2 BHK Luxe (318), 2 BHK Grande (310), 2 BHK + Home Office (310), 3 BHK Luxe (1,428), 3 BHK Grande (1,298), 4 BHK Luxe with three bathrooms (166) and 4 BHK Grande with four bathrooms (166).

What is the 2 BHK + Home Office at Sobha Hennur?

It is a two-bedroom home with an extra room planned as an office, the nearest thing at Sobha Hennur to a 2.5 BHK, and 310 are planned. The office keeps work apart from the bedrooms, which helps on days at home spent on calls. At launch, set its carpet area beside the 2 BHK Luxe and Grande to see what the extra room adds.

What is the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment?

It is one of the two compact formats, with 100 homes against 301 in the regular 1 BHK. Its indicative layout shows a bedroom, a living and dining area, a kitchen and a balcony, much like the 1 BHK, but in a serviced format. How the service will be run, what it costs and how it affects renting the home out are confirmed at launch, so ask about all three before choosing between the two.

How do the Luxe and Grande formats differ?

The names pair up across three sizes. In the 2 BHK, the Luxe and Grande layouts show the same rooms, and how they differ in area is set out at launch. In the 3 BHK, the Grande layout shows a wide balcony where the Luxe shows a balcony. In the 4 BHK, the Luxe has three bathrooms and the Grande four, with balconies rather than one. Compare their carpet areas, once published, before you pick one.

What are the sizes of Sobha Hennur homes?

The area of each home type has not been released; it comes with the price list at launch. The only area published so far is about 1,940 sq ft, the home that the indicative price of about ₹1.86 Cr refers to, and it does not say which home type that is or which area measure it uses. Ask for both the carpet area and the super built-up area of every home you shortlist.

What is the difference between carpet area and super built-up area?

Carpet area is the floor inside a home’s walls that you can actually use. Super built-up area is larger: it adds the thickness of the walls, the balconies and a proportion of the shared spaces, which at Sobha Hennur include the clubhouse and the amenity block. One flat can therefore carry two quite different figures. Compare homes on carpet area, and check which measure each price is charged on.

How tall are the towers, and does the floor matter?

The 13 towers rise to as many as 42 floors. How many floors each tower has, and which home types it carries, will be clear from the detailed plans at launch. The floor affects what you look out on, whether the parks or the lake edge on the east, and it may also change the price through a floor or view charge on the cost sheet, so ask for the same home type on a few different floors.

Can I get the detailed floor plans before launch?

The website shows indicative 3D layouts of all nine types, which give the rooms but not the dimensions. Dimensioned floor plans and the area of each home are released at launch. Register your home type through the form and the sales team will send them as soon as they are out, so you can check room sizes, the facing of the balcony and the bathroom arrangement before allotment.

Sobha Hennur Price, Payment and Home Loans

How much does a home at Sobha Hennur cost?

The indicative price is about ₹1.86 Cr for about 1,940 sq ft, with stamp duty and registration extra. Prices for each home type will be announced at launch; register your interest to receive the price list first.

What does the indicative price of about ₹1.86 Cr refer to?

It is the figure given for a home of about 1,940 sq ft, and it shows where Sobha Hennur is pitched before the price list is out. It is not tied to a named home type, so a 1 BHK and a 4 BHK will be priced very differently. Avoid working out a rate per sq ft from it and applying that to another home; wait for the launch price of the exact type you want.

When will the Sobha Hennur price list come out?

At launch. The price list will give each of the nine home types its own price, together with the areas, other charges and the payment plan. No launch date is published on this website. Register your interest with your preferred home type, and the sales team will send you the price list as soon as it is released, ahead of allotment.

What will a Sobha Hennur cost sheet show?

A cost sheet prices one specific home. Expect the base price and the area it is charged on, any floor or view charge, car parking, any clubhouse or amenity charge, maintenance deposits and taxes, with stamp duty and registration extra over the price. For the serviced 1 BHK, also look for any service charge. The total at the bottom, not the base rate, is the number to compare.

Is there a payment plan for Sobha Hennur?

The payment plan comes with the price list at launch. Homes bought before completion are usually paid for under a construction-linked plan: a booking amount, a payment at the agreement, then instalments as the building reaches set stages. When the Sobha Hennur plan arrives, see how many stages it has, whether they follow construction or fixed dates, and what proof of each stage you will receive.

Can I take a home loan for a Sobha Hennur home?

A home loan can cover part of the price, subject to the lender’s approval. In general, a bank lends a share of the price based on your income, your existing repayments and the home itself, and you pay the rest from savings. For a home under construction the loan is released in stages alongside the payment plan. An in-principle sanction before launch tells you your budget when the Sobha Hennur price list arrives.

Is car parking included in the price?

Sobha Hennur plans 5,029 parking spaces. Whether a space comes with your home or is priced separately, and how many spaces a home can have, will show on the cost sheet at launch. If you need two spaces, for example with a 4 BHK, say so when you register and ask for it to appear in writing on your cost sheet.

Sobha Hennur Location and Connectivity

Is Sobha Hennur on Hennur Road?

It is in the Hennur Road residential corridor, across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East, rather than on the road itself. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away and Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km.

How far is it from Manyata Tech Park and the airport?

Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km away and Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1, about 20.9 km. Thanisandra Railway Station is about 6.5 km away and Yelahanka Junction about 9.6 km.

What is the address of Sobha Hennur?

The project land is in Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk, in the Hennur Road corridor of North-East Bengaluru, Karnataka. A street address and postcode are not given on this website, so use the map link on the location page for directions, or ask the sales team for the meeting point when you book a visit.

Which schools and colleges are near Sobha Hennur?

VIBGYOR High School, Hennur is about 6.1 km away, and Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km. Inside the township, learning spaces with reading tables and bookshelves are planned for homework and quiet study. If your children attend a school that is not on our list, drive the route at the morning school hour before you choose a home, since that trip is made every day.

Is there a hospital close to Sobha Hennur?

The nearest hospital in the distance table is Cratis Hospital on Hennur Bagalur Main Road, about 5.4 km away, and Manipal Hospital, Hebbal is about 12.4 km away. Within the township, a health and first-aid room is planned for first aid and small health needs, so a minor problem need not mean a drive, though hospital care does.

How is Sobha Hennur connected for travel out of the city?

For rail, Thanisandra Railway Station is about 6.5 km away and Yelahanka Junction Railway Station about 9.6 km. For flights, Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1, is about 20.9 km by road. Towards the city, the Hebbal Flyover is about 15.3 km away. All are approximate driving distances, so check live travel times on the map at the hours you would really travel.

Sobha Hennur Amenities and Daily Life

What will the township include?

A clubhouse and an amenity block; space for learning, health, banking, post and communication, recreation and business; offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants; and parks and landscaped open spaces across about 37% of the land.

What is planned inside the Sobha Hennur clubhouse?

Pictures show the clubhouse lounge rising double height, with a window wall onto the gardens. Its sports and fitness facilities, and how its floors are divided, are confirmed at launch, and an amenity block is planned separately among the parks. Ask for the clubhouse floor plan and its expected opening, since a facility that opens long after you move in is of little use in the first years.

What everyday services are planned within the township?

A banking service point with an ATM and a counter covers routine banking, and a resident help desk with parcel lockers receives deliveries across the 13 towers. A health and first-aid room and space for post and communication are planned too. The commercial precinct at the main access adds shops and restaurants, so many small errands need not involve the main road at all.

Is there space for children to play and study?

Yes. Table tennis and carrom are among the indoor games planned, and the gallery shows a sports court and play lawn under shade trees. Learning spaces with reading tables and bookshelves give children a quiet place to study, and parks and open spaces run between the towers. Because the site is long, ask which play areas are nearest the block you are offered.

How green will Sobha Hennur be?

Green cover is planned on about 37% of the land. That includes parks and open spaces between the towers, landscaped gardens with a walk between lawns and trees, and a tree-planting programme. The master plan also shows a lake edge along the east of the site, nearest Blocks 6 to 12 around the loop road. Ask which parks your shortlisted tower faces.

What sustainability features does Sobha Hennur plan?

Waste water is to be treated on site in a sewage treatment plant using the activated sludge process, and dual plumbing keeps the treated water in separate pipes so it can be reused on site. Rainwater harvesting is planned too, along with the green cover and the tree-planting programme. Ask where the reused water will go and how drinking water will be supplied to your tower.

Is Sobha Hennur suited to working from home?

Three features help. The 2 BHK + Home Office gives 310 homes a separate office room. Business and meeting rooms are planned among the shared spaces for calls and meetings that need more than a desk at home. And offices are planned in the commercial precinct at the main access, inside the township itself and closest to Blocks 1 to 5.

Why choose a Sobha home?

As of June 2026 it had delivered 589+ developments and 152.69 million sq ft of built space, and it was building in 14 cities across India; its Bengaluru homes include SOBHA Neopolis, SOBHA Townpark and SOBHA OneWorld.

Which company is developing Sobha Hennur?

Sobha Limited, the Indian real estate company PNC Menon founded in 1995. Its shares trade on the NSE under the symbol SOBHA and on the BSE under the code 532784, so its results and announcements are public. It builds apartments, villas, row houses, plotted developments, commercial spaces and townships, of which Sobha Hennur is one. It is a separate company from Sobha Realty, a Dubai-based brand.
